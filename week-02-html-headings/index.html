Identify Your Skin Type

Before choosing a foundation, determine if your skin is dry, oily, combination, or normal. Dry skin often looks better with hydrating or luminous formulas, while oily skin may benefit from matte or oil-free foundations. Combination skin can require balanced formula what works well on both dry and oily areas.
