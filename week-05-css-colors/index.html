How we should study with AI nowadays

AI is becoming one of the best tools for learning. It's having a personal tutor available 24/7 to explain difficult concepts, improve your English, create practice tests, and help you study smarter. But there's a downside: Relying too much on AI can make us stop thinking critically, become dependent on technology, and lose the ability to solve problems independently.
